```bash
export CANON_AGENT_TEAMS_MODE=on
```

Default behavior is unchanged. Users who do not set this flag continue using the legacy state-machine orchestration. All v2.1a work is additive and gated behind this flag.

## Known limitations under v2.1a

### Cold-start latency floors

The planner's first-iteration latency has architectural floors that exceed the ideal user-patience window (15–30s):

- **REDIRECT (trivial/invalid requests):** ~20s floor, 30s median target
- **GREENLIGHT (trivial bug fixes):** ~30s floor, 60s median target
- **Non-trivial requests (migration, test-gap):** ~120s+ floor (architectural; driven by evidence-gathering requirements)

These floors are principled — the planner must gather evidence per `agent-evidence-over-intuition` — but they're above the patience threshold. v2_1b-09 tracks potential optimizations.

### Mandatory tail omission (NF-6)

Under compact output conditions (token pressure, high turn count, summarized preload), the planner may omit the mandatory tail steps (context-sync → learn) from synthesized runbooks. The core synthesis (step selection, confidence signals, contract pairings) remains correct. v2_1b-10 tracks enforcement.

### Confidence signal calibration (NF-5)

All validation scenarios produced HIGH confidence signals. This may indicate the confidence model lacks discrimination — a v2.2 calibration concern, not a v2.1a defect. Real-use soak data will reveal whether confidence signals are informative.

## Decision criteria for ending soak

The soak period ends when **both** conditions are met:

1. **≥ 20 synthesized runbooks** produced in real use (not validation or test runs)
2. **No new structural findings** filed during the soak period. If structural findings are filed, soak extends until they are triaged and either resolved or explicitly deferred to a later milestone.

When both conditions are met, v2.1b waves may begin per the v2.1b INDEX entry gate.
