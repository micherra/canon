## 12. New Findings

### NF-1: maxTurns exhaustion on complex scenarios (HIGH)

The planner exhausts its 40-turn budget on migration and test-gap scenarios without producing output. Root cause: the `agent-evidence-over-intuition` rule drives exhaustive investigation before synthesis. For complex scenarios with large investigation surfaces (multiple tables, multiple test files, cross-referencing contracts), the investigation phase alone exceeds the turn budget.

**Recommended remediation:** Add "synthesis budget" awareness to the planner — reserve at least 10 turns for brief+runbook production regardless of investigation depth. If investigation would exceed (maxTurns - 10), truncate investigation and proceed to synthesis with available evidence. Filed for v2.1b consideration.

### NF-2: Planner correctly rejects speculative refactors (informational)

Both refactor scenarios were REDIRECTed with evidence-based reasoning. The planner applied `agent-evidence-over-intuition`, `simplicity-first`, and `deep-modules` to reject refactors that lacked evidence of a real problem. This is correct behavior — it demonstrates the brief contract's quality filtering. Not a finding to fix; worth documenting as a validated behavior.

### NF-3: verify step `artifacts: []` schema deviation (LOW)

The S2 runbook's verify step has an empty `artifacts` list. The synthesis contract says "at least one path per step." The verify step produces a pass/fail outcome, not a file artifact. The contract may need an exception for outcome-only steps (verify, pre-launch-check). Filed as a minor contract clarification for v2.1b.

### NF-4: Latency scales non-linearly with request complexity (informational)

Trivial requests (spike): 19–51s. Non-trivial requests (validation): 121–193s. The planner's investigation depth is the dominant latency factor, scaling tool calls from 2–7 (trivial) to 18–39 (non-trivial). This is principled behavior per `agent-evidence-over-intuition` but means the revised targets (30s/60s) are only achievable for trivial requests. For non-trivial requests, the latency floor is ~120s. Already addressed by v2_1b-09 (planner efficiency follow-up).

### NF-5: Confidence signal overconfidence pattern (informational)

Both GREENLIGHT runbooks have all confidence signals at `high`. For genuinely trivial fixes (S1), this is defensible. For features with schema changes (S2), at least one dimension (e.g., risk or step-sequence) arguably warrants `medium`. Consistent with §13 risk "LLM overconfidence." The per-signal-only design (no aggregate scalar) limits the impact — users see individual rationales and can judge for themselves. No action needed in v2.1a; calibration is a v2.2 concern.
